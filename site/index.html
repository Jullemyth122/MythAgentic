01 — Samples

Eight made-up businesses. One needs no AI.

Four customer websites, a social video app with content moderation, and three staff handbooks where new employees learn the job: a coffee shop, a logistics warehouse and a call center. Scan one to see the placement map, then build its agent pack.

02 — Method

Code measures. AI explains. A formula decides.

The same site always gets the same answer. Every decision shows the signals that were measured and the rule that fired.

Priority
Friction×Value×Fit
Effort×Risk

Each part is 1 to 5, so Priority runs from under 1 to 125. An agent needs 20 or more and a Fit of 3 or more. Anything else gets a page fix, or nothing at all.

Five outcomes
  • Add an agentForm helper, answer desk, compare helper or an action other agents can call.
  • Plan nextWorth it, but the data lives in the business's own system first.
  • Fix the pagePublish the price, add the hours. Cheaper than any AI.
  • Fine as isNothing to do. Saying so builds trust.
  • Safety vetoPayments, health data, ID numbers, children. Whatever the score.
Pipeline
  1. MeasureFields, steps, missing labels, "price on request", hours, FAQs and logins, counted by code.
  2. ScoreFive parts, each with the reason it got its number.
  3. DecideSix placement rules and four vetoes, cited on every card.
  4. BuildNetwork, manifest and actions for approved spots, checked by the official validator.
