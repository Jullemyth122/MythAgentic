Policies and frequently asked questions

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel for free up to 24 hours before your tour. Cancellations within 24 hours are charged 50% of the tour price. If you do not show up at pickup time, the full price is charged.

What happens if the weather is bad?

The Coast Guard decides whether boats may leave. If tours are cancelled because of the weather, you can move to another day for free or get a full refund.

What should I bring?

Bring a towel, sunscreen that is safe for reefs, water shoes, a dry bag for your phone, and cash for drinks. Snorkel gear is included on most tours.

Can children join?

Children from age 4 can join with a parent. Children under 12 pay 70% of the adult price. Life jackets in child sizes are on every boat.

Is lunch included?

Lunch is included on tours A to D and on the private boat. The sunset cruise serves snacks. Tell us about allergies in the special requests box.

Can I join if I cannot swim?

Yes. Everyone wears a life jacket in the water, and the guide stays with guests who do not swim.

When is pickup?

Pickup starts at 8:00 AM for day tours and 3:30 PM for the sunset cruise. Your guide messages you the evening before with the exact time.

Do you accept card payments?

We accept cash only, paid at the pier before boarding.
