Exception codes

Scan an exception code when a parcel cannot go on as normal. Open a code for examples.

CodeMeaningWhen to use itWhat to doWho to tell
DMGDamagedThe box is torn, crushed, wet or leaking.Photograph it, move it to the red cage, and file a damage report.Shift supervisor
ADDAddress problemThe label is missing, unreadable or the address is incomplete.Move it to the blue shelf. Customer service calls the sender within 24 hours.Customer service desk
REFRefusedThe recipient refused the parcel at the door.Scan it as REF and stage it for return on the next linehaul.Dispatch lead
MISMissortedThe parcel arrived at the wrong hub or wrong sort lane.Rescan it, print a new route label, and put it on the correct lane.Sort lead
HLDOn holdCustoms, payment or a customer request holds the parcel.Stage it in the hold area. Do not dispatch until the hold is lifted in the system.Shift supervisor
RTSReturn to senderThree failed delivery attempts or a sender request.Scan it as RTS, bag it by sender, and stage it for the returns truck.Dispatch lead
