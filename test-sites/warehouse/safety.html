Safety questions

What new floor staff ask in their first week. Safety rules are not optional.

What do I need to wear on the floor?

Safety shoes, the reflective vest, and gloves when you unload. Hard hats in the racking area. No sandals, ever.

How heavy can I lift alone?

Up to 20 kg. Anything heavier is a two-person lift or goes on the pallet jack.

Can I walk in the forklift lane?

No. Walk only inside the green lines. Make eye contact with the forklift driver before you cross at a crossing.

What do I do with a leaking parcel?

Do not touch it with bare hands. Put on gloves, place it in the spill tray, and call the shift supervisor.

Where are the fire exits?

Exit A is next to Dock 1, Exit B is behind the returns cage. The meeting point is the parking lot gate.

How long are breaks on a hot day?

When the floor is above 32 °C, take a 10-minute water break every hour on top of your normal break.

Can I use a ladder to reach the top rack?

Only the rolling stair ladders with handrails, and only with someone holding it. Never climb the racks.

How do I report a near miss?

Tell the shift supervisor and write it in the near-miss log at the supervisor desk. Near misses are reported without blame.
