How receiving works

Every inbound truck is received at Dock 2. Follow these steps for each truck.

  1. Check the seal: Match the seal number on the truck door with the linehaul sheet before you open it. A broken seal goes to the shift supervisor first.
  2. Unload onto the conveyor: Labels face up. Heavy parcels (over 20 kg) go on the floor pallet, not the conveyor.
  3. Scan every parcel: Use the handheld scanner in Inbound mode. A beep and a green light mean the scan worked.
  4. Set aside exceptions: Any parcel that is damaged, unlabeled or refused gets an exception code. See the exception codes page.
  5. Close the truck in the system: When the truck is empty, close it on the scanner. The count must match the linehaul sheet.
  6. Report the difference: If the count does not match, write the difference on the sheet and tell the shift supervisor before the truck leaves.
