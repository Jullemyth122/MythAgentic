Moderator handbook

For new members of the Clipwave moderation team. Read it in your first week, alongside the rule breaks table. Practise on the review queue before your first shift. Removing real clips and giving strikes happens in the moderator console.

What does a normal shift look like?

A shift is 8 hours with a 15-minute break every 2 hours. You work through your review queue in order: reports marked "someone in danger" first, then automatic flags, then viewer reports. Aim for about 60 clips an hour, but never rush a hard one.

When do I remove a clip and when do I add a warning label?

Remove the clip when it breaks a rule. Add a warning label when it is allowed but upsetting, such as news footage or a trained stunt. If a label is enough, do not remove.

When do I mark a clip 18+ only instead of removing it?

When no rule is broken but the clip is not right for teen accounts: a jump-scare prank, strong language, or a stunt done safely by adults. Write what it contains in the label, like "18+: jump scare and strong language". No strike.

What do I do when I am not sure?

Choose "Second look" in the console and write one line about why. A senior moderator decides within 2 hours. Never remove a clip just to be safe.

What do I do when someone may be in danger?

Press "Urgent" in the console. The safety team on call takes it within 15 minutes and can contact local emergency services. Do not message the person yourself.

How do I write the removal note?

Name the rule, the moment in the clip, and what you saw, in one or two plain sentences. The creator reads this note, so keep it calm and factual. Example: "Harassment: at 0:12 the clip shows an edited photo of a classmate to mock them."

Can the AI assistant remove a clip for me?

No. On the practice queue the assistant can read a clip's report and timeline, find the rule it breaks, show you the moment, and fill in the decision form with a note. Nothing is applied until you press Apply decision and confirm. In the real console, removing a clip and giving a strike are always done by you, after you have watched the clip.

How do I look after myself?

Some clips are hard to watch. Take your breaks, switch to the copyright queue for an hour when you need to, and talk to the wellbeing team any time. Your team lead will never count a break against you.

Who checks my decisions?

Each week a senior moderator re-checks 20 of your decisions. Appeals are read by a different moderator. If many of your removals are overturned, your team lead will go through the rules with you again.
