Community guidelines

These rules apply to every clip, caption, comment and live stream on Clipwave. When a clip breaks a rule, a moderator removes it or adds a warning label, and the creator may get a strike.

Can I use popular music in my clip?

Yes, if you pick it from the Clipwave sound library. Those songs are licensed for clips. Music you add from outside the library may be muted or removed when the rights owner asks. Three copyright removals in 90 days close the account.

What counts as harassment?

Clips that insult, threaten or shame a real person, tag them to pile on, or share edited images of them to mock them. Disagreeing with someone's opinion is not harassment. Repeated insults aimed at one person are, even if each one seems small.

Can I film a stunt or a dangerous challenge?

Stunts done by trained people with safety gear are allowed with a warning label. Challenges that invite viewers to copy something dangerous, such as holding their breath, eating detergent or jumping from moving cars, are removed straight away.

Is it okay to show someone's home address or phone number?

No. Showing private information about anyone, including addresses, phone numbers, school names, car plates or documents, is removed even when it was an accident. Blur it and post again.

Can I run a giveaway?

Yes, if you say who runs it, what the prize is and when it ends, and you never ask people to pay or to send card details to enter. Fake giveaways and "send money to win" clips are scams and are removed.

What happens when my clip is removed?

You get a removal notice in your inbox with the rule it broke and a notice number. Most rule breaks also give one strike. You can appeal within 30 days from the appeal page, using the notice number.

How many strikes before my account is closed?

Strikes last 90 days. One strike: a warning. Two strikes: no posting for 7 days. Three strikes: the account is closed. Severe rule breaks, such as threats of violence or scams, can close an account with the first strike.

Can I post clips made with AI?

Yes, if you label them as made with AI. Posting the same AI-made clip again and again, or from many new accounts, to farm views is spam: it is removed and the accounts are closed. So are made-up "facts" with no source posted as real.

Who sees clips marked 18+?

Some clips are allowed but not right for younger viewers: horror pranks, strong language, or risky stunts done safely by adults. A moderator marks them 18+ only. Teen accounts never see them, and they are left out of the For You feed for people who are not signed in.

Can I post news footage that shows violence?

News and documentary clips may show violence when the caption gives context and the clip is not made to celebrate it. These clips get a warning label and are not shown in the For You feed.

Does a person or a computer decide when my clip breaks a rule?

Automatic checks flag clips that might break a rule, and viewer reports flag more. A human moderator looks at every flagged clip before it is removed or a strike is given. Appeals are always read by a different moderator.
