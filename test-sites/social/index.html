Clipwave: short videos, kind community

Clipwave is a place to share clips up to 90 seconds long. Millions of clips go up every day (made up), so we keep the community safe with clear rules, reports from viewers, and a team of human moderators.

Keeping Clipwave safe

  • Community guidelines: what you can and cannot post.
  • Rule breaks: the six kinds of rule break and what happens to each.
  • Report a clip that breaks the rules.
  • Appeal a removal if we took down your clip by mistake.

For our moderation team

New moderators start with the moderator handbook, then practise on the review queue: eight made-up reported clips to watch and decide. Removing real clips happens in the moderator console, which needs a moderator sign-in.
