Insurance coverage and frequently asked questions

Do you accept dental insurance?

We accept selected insurance plans for some services. Coverage depends on your insurance plan, the service, and whether your company plan includes dental benefits. Some plans cover only consultation and cleaning once a year, while other plans also cover simple fillings and extractions. Please bring your insurance card and a valid ID on the day of your visit, and we will check your coverage with your insurance before treatment. Approval can take up to thirty minutes during busy hours.

Which insurance providers do you accept?

We currently accept cards from several major insurance providers in the area. The list changes from time to time when contracts are renewed, so please ask the front desk before your visit. Some providers are accepted only at the Downtown branch.

Is cleaning covered by my insurance?

Most dental plans cover one oral prophylaxis (cleaning) per year. Deep cleaning, also called scaling and root planing, is usually not covered and is charged separately. If your plan has a yearly limit, any amount above the limit is paid by you.

Are braces covered?

Braces are usually not covered by insurance plans. We offer installment plans for braces; ask our staff for details during your consultation.

Do you accept walk-in patients?

Walk-in patients are welcome when there are free slots, but patients with bookings are served first. We recommend booking ahead, especially on Saturdays.

Can I reschedule my booking?

Yes. Please call us at least one day before your visit to reschedule. If you do not arrive within fifteen minutes of your booked time, your slot may be given to another patient.

Do you treat children?

Yes, our dentists see children from age three. A parent or guardian must stay with the child during the visit.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, debit and credit cards, and e-wallets at the front desk. Payment is made after treatment.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a valid ID, your insurance card if you have one, and any recent dental X-rays. Arrive ten minutes early to fill out the patient information sheet.

Is parking available?

The Downtown branch has limited parking in front of the building. The Riverside branch is inside a mall with paid parking.
