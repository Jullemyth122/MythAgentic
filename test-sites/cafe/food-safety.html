Food safety questions

Answers to what new crew ask most. If something is not here, ask the shift lead before you guess.

What temperature should the fridges be?

Between 1 and 4 °C. Check both fridges at opening, at 2:00 PM and at closing, and write the readings in the temperature log. Above 5 °C, tell the shift lead right away.

How long is opened milk good for?

Fresh milk is good for 3 days after opening, oat milk for 7 days. Write the opening date on the carton with a marker.

How do I handle a guest's allergy?

Ask what they are allergic to, check the build card's allergens, and use a clean pitcher and steam wand. If you are not sure, offer a drink without it and tell the shift lead.

When do I wash my hands?

When you start your shift, after the toilet, after handling cash or trash, after touching your face or phone, and before handling food. Wash for 20 seconds.

What do I do with a dropped pastry?

Throw it away and write it in the waste log. Never put it back in the case.

How do I mix the sanitizer?

One capful of sanitizer to 4 liters of water in the red bucket. Change it every 4 hours.

What if I see a pest?

Do not spray anything. Close the area if you can, take a photo, and call the shift lead and the store manager.

What do I do if I get a small burn from the steam wand?

Cool it under running water for 10 minutes, then tell the shift lead and write it in the incident log. For anything bigger, call the store manager.
