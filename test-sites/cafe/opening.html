Opening checklist (6:00 AM)

Two crew open the store. Work through the steps in order and tick them on the clipboard by the register.

  1. Unlock and switch off the alarm: Use the side door. Enter the alarm code from the shift lead within 30 seconds.
  2. Turn on the espresso machine: It needs 25 minutes to heat. Check the pressure gauge reads 9 bar before the first shot.
  3. Check fridge temperatures: Both fridges must read 1 to 4 °C. Write the readings in the temperature log.
  4. Calibrate the grinder: Pull a test double shot: 18 g in, 36 g out, in 25 to 30 seconds. Adjust the grind if it runs fast or slow.
  5. Set up the pastry case: Take pastries from the delivery box, check the delivery date, and label each tray with today's date.
  6. Count the cash drawer: The opening float is $300 in small bills and coins. Count it with the second crew member.
  7. Open the door at 7:00 AM: Switch the sign to Open and turn on the music.
