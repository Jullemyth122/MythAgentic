Caramel macchiato

Sizes: 12 oz / 16 oz. Espresso shots: 1 / 2. Milk: Fresh milk. Syrup or base: Vanilla, 15 ml / 20 ml, plus caramel drizzle.

How to build it

  1. Pump vanilla into the cup.
  2. Steam the milk and pour it in first.
  3. Pull the shots and pour them on top so they mark the milk.
  4. Drizzle caramel in a crosshatch.

Allergens

Contains milk. Always ask the guest about allergies when they order a milk drink.
