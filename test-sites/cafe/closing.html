Closing checklist (9:30 PM)

  1. Last orders at 9:45 PM: Tell guests politely; no new hot food after 9:30 PM.
  2. Backflush the espresso machine: Use one scoop of cleaner in the blind basket, five cycles of 10 seconds.
  3. Clean the steam wands: Purge, wipe, and soak the tips in hot water for 10 minutes.
  4. Store milk and pastries: Date-label open milk. Pastries older than one day go to the waste log.
  5. Count the cash drawer: Leave the $300 float. Put the rest in the drop safe with the shift report.
  6. Mop and take out the trash: Mop from the back to the door. Trash goes to the bins behind the building.
  7. Set the alarm and lock up: Two crew leave together. Text the shift lead when you have locked the door.
