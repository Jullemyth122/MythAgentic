Data privacy request

The customer asks what data is kept about them, or asks to delete it. Tier 3, response within 24 hours, hand off to Data protection officer.

Before you escalate

  1. Do not read out account data beyond what you need to verify them.
  2. Log the request with the customer's exact words.
  3. Send it to the data protection officer queue; never promise a deletion yourself.
