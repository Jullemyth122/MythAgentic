Complaint against an agent

The customer complains about how a previous agent treated them. Tier 2, response within 4 hours, hand off to Team lead on duty.

Before you escalate

  1. Apologize and listen without arguing.
  2. Write what happened, with dates and agent names if given.
  3. Escalate to the team lead on duty before the call ends.
