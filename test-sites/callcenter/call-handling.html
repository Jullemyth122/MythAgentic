Call handling questions

The questions new agents ask most in nesting. Your team lead has the final word.

What is the greeting?

"Thank you for calling Northstar, this is (your name). May I have your account number?" Smile while you say it; callers hear it.

How do I verify the caller?

Ask for the account number, then two of: the registered name, the billing address, or the last payment amount. Never ask for a full card number.

How long can I put a caller on hold?

Two minutes at most. Ask permission first, and come back every 60 seconds to say you are still working on it.

What do I do about dead air?

Never stay silent for more than 10 seconds. Say what you are doing: "I'm checking your last bill now."

How do I handle an angry caller?

Let them finish, apologize for the experience, repeat the problem back, and say what you will do next. Do not take it personally; ask your team lead to listen in if you need help.

When do I transfer a call?

Only when the escalation matrix says the issue belongs to another team. Tell the caller who they will talk to and why, then do a warm transfer.

What is the average handle time target?

6 minutes for nesting agents, 5 minutes after nesting. Quality comes first; never rush a caller to meet it.

Do I tell the caller the call is recorded?

Yes, the IVR tells them, and you repeat it if they ask. Never record calls on your own phone.

How do I end the call?

Recap what you did, give the ticket number if there is one, ask "Is there anything else I can help you with?", and thank them for calling Northstar.
